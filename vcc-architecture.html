Governance Framework · 治理框架

1. MAS · Regulatory Oversight
Grants fund management licence · Approves VCC incorporation · Administers 13O/13U tax incentive
2. Olive GP · Investment Manager
Portfolio construction · Asset allocation · Risk management · Fiduciary duty to LP
3. VCC · Legal Segregation
s.29 VCCA statutory ring-fencing · Each SF: segregated assets, liabilities, and books
4. Fund Admin · Outsourced Middle/Back Office
Fund accounting · Investor servicing · Regulatory reporting · Independent valuation
5. Custodian · Asset Safekeeping
Per-SF segregated custody accounts · Cash monitoring · Trade settlement
6. LP · Limited Partner
Liability limited to committed capital · No cross-SF recourse · Subscription Agreement governs
